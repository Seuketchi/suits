# What the associates copies: real roles and real teams

The associates build, test first, each on its own files, under louis. This page names where that comes from.

Drawn from the research notes of 6 November 2026 (`cast-rrl.md` and `team-work-rrl.md`). The sources below were read for those notes; they were not re-read for this page. VERIFIED means the sentence was read on the page named. UNCONFIRMED means a summary only, a conflict, or not found.

## From the show

- Louis is "the direct supervisor of the firm's associate attorneys". VERIFIED, [List of Suits characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Suits_characters). That is the only show source: no named associate was checked, and the group needs no more.

## From the real job

- No profession source was used. A developer's job is not a legal associate's, so the working rules come from team practice below.

## From how real teams work

- **Small batches.** [DORA capabilities](https://dora.dev/capabilities/) (working in small batches): small changes shorten feedback and make problems easier to triage.
- **Limit work in progress.** DORA (WIP limits): limits expose problems, and too much at once makes tasks slower and burns the team out. The limit of three at once is this team's choice, not a published number.
- **One modifier per area.** [Google SRE book, managing incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/): during an incident "the operations team should be the only group modifying the system". Each associate owns its files and touches no one else's.
- **Done means the test.** [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html): an item that fails the definition of done goes back. Tests are written first and seen to fail.
- **Stop at the budget.** [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup): the default after a miss is to stop, not to extend. Three failed fixes go to harvey.
- **Methods.** Test-first building and the diagnosis loop come from Matt Pocock's skills (MIT).
