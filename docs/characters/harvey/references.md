# What harvey copies: real roles and real teams

Harvey decides at the front and closes only exceptions. This page names where that comes from.

Drawn from the research notes of 6 November 2026 (`cast-rrl.md` and `team-work-rrl.md`). The sources below were read for those notes; they were not re-read for this page. VERIFIED means the sentence was read on the page named. UNCONFIRMED means a summary only, a conflict, or not found.

## From the show

- Known as a "closer", "skilled at negotiation"; cases are resolved "through negotiation, courtroom strategy, or careful planning". VERIFIED, Harvey Specter on Wikipedia.
- Mentor and immediate superior to Mike, and assigns him cases. VERIFIED, [List of Suits characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Suits_characters).
- Not found: Harvey reviewing other people's work as a job. His stated strength is closing and negotiating, so the character decides and closes and does not review. UNCONFIRMED as a show fact; it is the reason the review sits with louis.

## From the real job

- Lawyers, on [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org), list Judgment and Decision Making, Negotiation and Persuasion among their skills.
- The Harvard Program on Negotiation names interests, options and the best alternative (BATNA) as the base of preparation. Only the article teaser was read.

## From how real teams work

- **One approver.** [Atlassian DACI](https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci): the approver is "the one person (yes: one!) who makes the decision"; contributors have "a voice, but not a vote". Mike and rachel advise harvey and do not vote.
- **Escalation by case.** [GitLab directly responsible individuals](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/directly-responsible-individuals/): decisions that involve three or more functions, could have a large financial impact, or could present significant risk leave the individual owner. These are the cases that reach harvey.
- **No sign-off on every request.** [DORA capabilities](https://dora.dev/capabilities/) (streamlining change approval): approval is best done by peer review during development, and no evidence supports an external gate lowering failures.
- **Decide at the front, with a budget.** [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup): work is bet on before the cycle, and a miss stops the work by default instead of extending it.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Google's review guide: "Technical facts and data overrule opinions and personal preferences."
