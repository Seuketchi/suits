# What jessica copies: real roles and real teams

Jessica plans, tracks and holds the budget. This page names where that comes from, and where the fit is loose.

Drawn from the research notes of 6 November 2026 (`cast-rrl.md` and `team-work-rrl.md`). The sources below were read for those notes; they were not re-read for this page. VERIFIED means the sentence was read on the page named. UNCONFIRMED means a summary only, a conflict, or not found.

## From the show

- "The firm's wise but Machiavellian managing partner". "When faced with a dilemma, she will choose the less risky choice." VERIFIED, [List of Suits characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Suits_characters).
- Louis is trusted with her instructions "carried out precisely and without question". VERIFIED, same page.
- Not found: Jessica scheduling, budgeting, tracking deliverables or coordinating a project. The show gives that work to Donna as chief operating officer. The fit as a project manager is loose; the owner chose to keep it. UNCONFIRMED as a show fact.

## From the real job

- The nearest listing on [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org) to a managing partner is Chief Executives (11-1011.00): "Determine and formulate policies and provide overall direction", including budget and financial activity.
- Project Management Specialists (13-1082.00) on [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org) stand in for the project manager's duties; the Project Management Institute's own page could not be read.

## From how real teams work

- **Hand over a problem with boundaries, not a task list.** [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup): "We don't start by assigning tasks to anyone." The team works within the boundaries of the bet. She hands over a bounded goal with a budget.
- **A miss stops the work.** [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup): cancel work that does not ship in the cycle by default. This is the stop rule.
- **Watch blocked and aged items.** [Kanban Guide](https://kanbanguides.org/english/): active management includes "Unblocking blocked work" and ensuring items do not age unnecessarily.
- **A dot that does not move is a raised hand.** [Shape Up](https://basecamp.com/shapeup): a stuck item should show as one.
- **Command only on an incident.** [Google SRE book, managing incidents](https://sre.google/sre-book/managing-incidents/): declare early, keep one living document, and the commander holds the roles not delegated.
- **One accountable owner per task.** [GitLab directly responsible individuals](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/directly-responsible-individuals/): only one person is accountable for each task.
- **Work in progress is limited.** DORA: limits expose problems in the system, and without them "the team burns out".
