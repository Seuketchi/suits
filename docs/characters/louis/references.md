# What louis copies: real roles and real teams

Louis checks work against the rules and the spec, and supervises the builders. This page names where that comes from.

Drawn from the research notes of 6 November 2026 (`cast-rrl.md` and `team-work-rrl.md`). The sources below were read for those notes; they were not re-read for this page. VERIFIED means the sentence was read on the page named. UNCONFIRMED means a summary only, a conflict, or not found.

## From the show

- "The firm's leading authority and expert on all financial matters and the direct supervisor of the firm's associate attorneys". VERIFIED, [List of Suits characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Suits_characters).
- Trusted with work that must be carried out "precisely and without question", and the firm's highest billing attorney by hours. VERIFIED, same page.
- Not found: Louis auditing or reviewing Mike's work by name. His supervision of associates is stated; a review step is inferred. UNCONFIRMED.

## From the real job

- Accountants and auditors, on [O*NET OnLine](https://www.onetonline.org) (13-2011.00), "examine accounting records and statements for accuracy and compliance" and "review accounts for discrepancies". Cautiousness, integrity and attention to detail are listed as work styles (a secondary summary).
- The Institute of Internal Auditors makes independence and objectivity central. The reviewer is not the author of what is reviewed, which is why louis reviews work he did not write and edits none of it.

## From how real teams work

- **The done test is written up front.** [Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html): if an item does not meet the definition of done, "it cannot be released or even presented", and it returns to the backlog. Louis gets the done test at intake.
- **Review the whole, not for perfection.** Google's review guide: approve once the change "definitely improves the overall code health", even if it is not perfect. The most important thing to cover is the overall design.
- **Small and quick.** Google: respond within one business day at most; about 100 lines is a reasonable change and 1000 is usually too large. Louis checks pieces as they arrive.
- **Disagreement has a path.** Agree, record it, then escalate; never let it sit. Three failed rounds go to harvey.
- **Peer review beats a gate at the end.** [DORA capabilities](https://dora.dev/capabilities/): peer review during development, not an external approval step.
