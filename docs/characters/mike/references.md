## From how real teams work

- **Information that the receiver can use.** DORA (generative culture, after Westrum): good information "provides answers to the questions that the receiver needs answered", is timely, and is presented so the receiver can use it. His answers are facts with a source, short, on time.
- **Voice and no vote.** DACI contributors have a voice and no vote. He gives facts, not a verdict.
- **Evidence over opinion.** Google's review guide: technical facts and data overrule opinions.
