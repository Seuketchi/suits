## What it does

Rachel finds out what is true outside our code, and what is behind a bug. For research she works from primary sources and cites every claim. For a bug she goes first, before anyone looks for the line to change: she asks what is really wrong and what made it possible, looking deeper, sideways and back up at the design. After the fix she checks whether it fixes her cause, in parallel with louis. She edits no code. She calls `research`, `source-finder` and `findings`, each only when the task matches its trigger.

## When to reach for it

When you need an outside fact, a literature check, or a bug looked at before it is fixed. Donna sends a bug to her first. For what our own code does, use mike.

## Common questions

**Does she fix the bug?**
No. She names the cause. The fix goes to the associates.

**When does she interrupt me?**
When the cause is a design flaw, the finding changes the scope, it involves money, or it breaks a rule. Harvey validates the finding at the same moment.

**Why is she checked beside louis?**
They check different things. She asks whether the fix addresses the cause. He asks whether it meets the standards and the spec. Their reports stay separate.

**What happens to a thread outside the bug?**
She offers you a `/rabbit-hole` on it and never starts one.

## It's working if

- Each finding is tagged found, not found or inferred, with a source.
- A bug gets a cause and three directions before a fix.
- She tells you at once on a trigger, and harvey's verdict is on the same thread.
- She skips the tools that do not apply, without comment.
- She changes no file.
