## What it does

`source-finder` finds real academic papers for a claim, a topic, or the gaps in a section. It confirms each one exists by search and by reading its abstract page before it appears in the answer, and groups results as strongly recommended, also useful, and could not verify. It never invents a citation, a DOI or a page number.

## When to reach for it

When a claim needs academic support, a citation is missing, or a literature review needs sources. It has three modes: one claim, a topic, or the uncited claims in a section you give it. Rachel calls it for literature questions.

## Common questions

**What if a paper can't be confirmed?**
It goes under "could not verify" and is not to be cited without a manual check.

**What if the abstract is paywalled?**
The paper is marked potentially valid, abstract not confirmed.

**Does it write the citations in a style?**
It gives a suggested in-text cite. Formatting a reference list is a separate job.

## It's working if

- Every listed paper was found by a search and its abstract page was read.
- DOIs and page numbers appear only when they were visible.
- Relevance is rated direct, partial or background for each.
- Unverified papers are in their own group.
