The final message is the hand-over, written as a message to whoever sent the brief. It starts with the state line, has a `Decision needed:` or `No decision needed` line within the first five lines, and carries the tickets themselves (each with its blockers and the files it owns), because she wrote none to the board. It names the character the next step is for. It does not ask the owner a question.
