# Minutes and action log

From the owner's notes or a transcript, draft the minutes, then append the actions to the one action log. Out within one working day of the meeting, and the packet to the attendees at least 24 hours before the next one: the owner sends it, donna never does.

**Needs:** the notes or transcript (a file or pasted text), the attendee list, the date, time and place. Never invent what was said: a gap is a placeholder and a question. Minutes of a meeting that did not happen, or backdated, are refused.

## The minutes

For a board or stockholder meeting of a Philippine corporation, the minimum is the Revised Corporation Code, Sec. 73(g) (VERIFIED, primary): the time and place, how the meeting was authorised, the notice given, the agenda, regular or special and its object if special, those present and absent, and every act done or ordered done. On demand: when a member entered or left, the yeas and nays, and any protest in full. The wording for a corporate meeting is in [LEGAL.md](../docs/LEGAL.md), kind 5. Parts of an ordinary meeting:

1. Title, date, start and end time, place or platform.
2. Present, absent, in attendance.
3. Each agenda item: what was discussed in two lines, **the decision** in a sentence (or "no decision"), and any vote.
4. Actions, as a table: **Owner, Task, Due** (a calendar date, never "next week").
5. Next meeting, and adjournment time.
6. Prepared by and the date; approved by, after the next meeting adopts it.

Decisions, not a transcript: 1 to 3 pages. Written in the plain professional register: past tense, no praise, no "it was agreed that" where it was not.

## The action log

One file for the whole engagement, with one row per action. Donna is its only writer; `jessica` reads it ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md), "Working with jessica"). There is never a second copy.

| ID | Owner | Task | Due | Status | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-014 | Maria Santos | Send the signed UAT results | 18 November 2026 | open | Minutes of 6 November | |

- **Status** is `open`, `done`, `dropped` or `parked` (the owner paused it; the resurface date is in Notes and in the tickler, [TICKLER.md](TICKLER.md)). A due date moved, a task changed or a status closed is a **dated line in Notes**, never an overwrite of the row's first text.
- The log lives at `donna/action-log.md` in the owner's repo or notes, or where the owner says. Look for an existing log first and extend it; never start a second one. When a desk server is connected (tools `desk_log` and `desk_read`), the log is the desk's `action` log instead, one entry per row, a dated change as a new entry that `corrects` the row.
- Each row names its source (the minutes it came from) so a reader can trace it.
- The tickler ([TICKLER.md](TICKLER.md)) reads this file for what is due.

## Rules

- **Never change the record after the fact.** Minutes are corrected by a dated amendment appended at the foot, never overwritten (the Code requires the record to be carefully made; the rule on amendment is an inference, UNCONFIRMED).
- A decision reaches `jessica` as a line for the decision log: the decision, who made it, the date, the minutes it came from.
- Do not put an action on a person who was not asked: an action without a taker is a question to the owner.
- Confidential: keep names, board material and personal data in the owner's files.

## Standard

Minutes final within 24 hours, action items in a fixed Owner, Task, Due shape and the packet out 24 hours before the next meeting are practitioner opinion (Granola's guide to meeting minutes: VERIFIED, secondary). The chartered governance institute's factsheet names "accurately recording board decisions" and "monitoring action points" (VERIFIED, primary). O\*NET lists attending meetings to record minutes (63) and compiling, transcribing and distributing them (65).
