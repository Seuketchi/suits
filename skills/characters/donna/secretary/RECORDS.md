# Records register and retention check

A register of the documents that matter (contracts, NDAs, approvals, change records, board papers): where each lives, who owns it, its date, its retention class and when it may go. Donna flags a missing, unsigned or expired one. She does not delete or move a file.

**Needs:** the files, and the retention periods from a person: the bank client's compliance or the owner's counsel set them, from the client's policy and the law. **Donna never invents a retention period.** An empty period is a question.

## The register

`donna/records.md` in the owner's repo or notes, or where the owner says. Look for an existing one first and extend it.

| Record | Kind | Where it lives | Owner | Dated | Retention class | Keep until | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NDA, Example Corp and Sample Services | contract | `contracts/nda-2026.pdf` | Owner | 6 November 2026 | [set by compliance] | [not set] | signed copy filed |

- **Kind:** contract, NDA, approval, change record, board paper, minutes, other.
- **Where it lives** is a real path or link: check it exists. A record that is in an inbox only is flagged.
- **Retention class** and **Keep until** come from a person. Until set, the cell reads `[not set]` and the check lists it.
- **Status:** signed copy filed, unsigned, draft only, superseded by (the newer record), expired.

## The check

Run it on request and in the weekly brief ([BRIEFING.md](BRIEFING.md)). One line per finding, verdict first:

1. **Missing:** the path does not exist.
2. **Unsigned or draft only** where a signed copy is needed.
3. **No retention class** set.
4. **Past its keep-until date:** may now go, for a person to decide. Donna proposes; the owner or compliance disposes.
5. **Expiring** within 60 days: a contract term, a validity date. (60 days is a default.)

## Rules

- Never delete, move, rename or overwrite a record. Never mark one destroyed.
- The register holds **names and places, not contents**: no personal data and no credentials in a cell. A secret is never typed into it.
- Company and customer material stays inside the owner's files ([SKILL.md](../SKILL.md), hard rules).
- A corporation's own records (articles, bylaws, directors and officers, resolutions, minutes, the SEC reports) are those the Revised Corporation Code, Sec. 73, requires at its principal office, kept "regardless of the form in which they are stored" (VERIFIED, primary). Donna's register is the owner's, not the corporation's: for a corporate secretary's records, the duty is the secretary's.

## Standard

O\*NET lists filing and retrieving corporate documents (70) and maintaining a records management database (72) (VERIFIED, primary). A retention schedule has no source opened here: the National Archives rules and the BIR and SEC retention periods are the places to check: UNCONFIRMED. Never quote a period from this file.
